Privacy, plainly.

hannai is an independent application in development, maintained through the js503 GitHub account. Live Etsy integration is not yet publicly available.

This website

This site contains no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or contact forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical information such as IP addresses under GitHub’s privacy statement. External links take you to services with their own policies.

Your artwork and local files

The app imports artwork from files you choose and generates previews on your device. It preserves the original artwork for the customer download. Local job data includes images, listing details, settings and file hashes. Artwork is not sent to an AI service during listing generation. Reusable scene backgrounds may be AI-authored in advance.

Optional Etsy connection

When enabled, Etsy sign-in authorizes access to your shop. The connection service stores separate authorization tokens in the operating system’s credential store. Your Etsy password is entered on Etsy, not in hannai. The app stores its own connection credential in the device Keychain.

Only after you approve an upload does the service receive the listing images, customer ZIP and listing details required to create a draft. That ZIP includes your original artwork and license. The service also keeps upload receipts and file hashes to prevent duplicate work and help recover interrupted uploads. Google Drive is not part of this iteration.

Retention and control

Local files remain until removed. Jobs with delivery records are protected from accidental deletion during recovery. The private test service retains uploaded packages and receipts; it does not currently have an automatic deletion schedule. Before a wider release, the service’s retention and deletion process will be documented here. Disconnecting removes the service’s saved Etsy tokens; it does not delete Etsy listings or uploaded packages. Access can also be revoked through Etsy.

Questions and requests

Contact the maintainer through the hannai repository to arrange support or a data request. GitHub issues are public: do not post artwork, passwords, API credentials or other private information. No sale of personal data or advertising use is part of hannai’s current design.
